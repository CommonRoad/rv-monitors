\subsection{Precedence and Associativity}
We define the operator precedences of an LTL formula as follows: Unary operators have precedence over binary connectives with \until and \release binding stronger than $\rightarrow$, $\lor$, and $\land$. \strongNext has a higher precedence than \globally and \future, which both bind equally strong. The connective $\neg$ has the highest precedence. Table \ref{prec} illustrates the precedence relations including the respective associativity. By left associative we mean that the operator is evaluated from left to right, e.g., $\varphi_1\land \varphi_2 \land \varphi_3 := (\varphi_1\land \varphi_2) \land \varphi_3$. Analogously, right-associative operators such as \until are evaluated from right to left: $\varphi_1$\until$\varphi_2$\until$\varphi_3 := \varphi_1$\until$(\varphi_2$\until$\varphi_3)$.
